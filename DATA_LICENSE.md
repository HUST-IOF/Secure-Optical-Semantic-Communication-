# Experimental data

The selected measurements and historical result tables are supplied by the
research authors for inspection and reproduction of the accompanying examples.
The source-code Apache license does not establish a separate open-data license
for these experimental records. No additional broad data license has been
selected in this release. Contact the corresponding authors about other reuse.

The measurement files are unchanged from the selected source acquisitions.
Their provenance and checksums are documented under `data/measured/`.
The sample calibration responses are public demonstration data, and must not
be treated as secret keys in an operational communication system.
